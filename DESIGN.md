# Design System: Battle Pass ROI Calculator

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Official Strategy Guide"**

The system is a printed strategy guide for the reader's own playtime: coated cool-white stock run through a four-color press. Cyan, magenta and rich black are not accents; each one owns a whole chapter as a full-bleed plate, and where two inks meet they overprint by multiply, exactly as wet ink would. Crop marks, registration targets, color control strips and halftone dot ramps are the printer's furniture, and they stay visible because they are the world, not decoration on top of it.

Density is a guidebook's density: enormous extended display words set against tight ruled stat tables in tabular figures. Scale courage carries the hierarchy; a single verdict word fits the full width of its column while the numbers beside it sit at table size. One fluorescent spot ink, the highlighter, marks only what is in the reader's hand: their inputs, their edits, their selection. Every figure carries a provenance tag (yours, est., derived) so no estimate passes as fact, and every state prints itself as a sentence in the guide's voice instead of as a pill or badge.

The world refuses the category default of a dark neon gaming dashboard with glass cards and gradient headings. Motion is a press run: plates slide into register, misregister when a figure changes, and snap back on an exponential ease-out.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool paper ground, rich-black ink, process cyan and magenta as chapter plates.
- Multiply overprint wherever two inks meet, at rest and in motion.
- Anybody 900 uppercase on a live width axis for display; Archivo with tabular figures for text and tables.
- Ruled tables, hairline and heavy rules, almost no containers.
- Highlighter yellow-green reserved for the reader's own numbers.
- Provenance tags on every figure.
- One 12-column grid that tabs, tables and plates all snap to.
- Book furniture on every chapter opener: a running head with spread folios and an "In this chapter" contents block.

## Colors

Four process inks on cool paper, plus one fluorescent spot ink that belongs to the reader.

### Primary
- **Rich Black Ink** (ink): All running text, rules, table hairlines, primary buttons, the tier dots, and the Bestiary plate. It is the guide's voice; 3px and 7px rules in this ink carry the structure.
- **Process Cyan** (process-cyan): The Walkthrough chapter plate and tab, the reachable stretch of the route map trail, and reached tier nodes. Text on cyan is Rich Black Ink, never white.
- **Process Magenta** (process-magenta): The Shop Math chapter plate and tab, the ledger operators, wanted-item tiles, the sticker-ROI strike, the "for you" ROI rule and the season-end wall on the route map. White (on-magenta) is the only text color set directly on the magenta plate.

### Secondary
- **Magenta Text Ink** (magenta-ink): Magenta as text on paper: the wall label, unmet clear-condition states, and "probably not" verdict cells. Process Magenta stays a surface ink; this darker pull is what passes AA as type.
- **Process Yellow** (process-yellow): Press-check plates only: the yellow plate of the opening title and the yellow patch of the control strips. It never appears in the reading experience.
- **Control Strip Patches** (strip-blue, strip-red, strip-green, strip-gray-dark, strip-gray, strip-gray-light): The extra patches of the color control strips, after the four process inks: a blue, red and green overprint check and a three-step gray balance. They appear only in the press-check bar and the colophon color bar (see The Press Furniture Rule).

### Tertiary
- **Highlighter** (highlighter): The reader's own inputs and nothing else: the swiped marker under the sentence inputs, the "yours" tag, the slider thumb, edited appendix fields, checked clear-condition boxes, the current game row in the comparison table, the highlighted hours in bestiary entries, text selection, and the focus ring on the black plate.

### Neutral
- **Coated Paper** (paper): Page ground, input fills, ghost-surface fills, text on the black plate.
- **Paper Tint** (paper-2): Hover fill for ghost buttons and comparison rows, the verdict tab, scrollbar track.
- **Paper Shade** (paper-3): The Appendix plate and tab, empty slider track.
- **Ink 2** (ink-2): Secondary text: table row heads, notes, captions, sources.
- **Ink 3** (ink-3): Tertiary text: hints, keys, unit marks, the derived-tag border, dotted missed tiers on the tier sheet, the dashed stroke of missed route nodes, and the dots of the halftone screen that fills locked tiles.
- **Ink Lift** (ink-lift): Hover state of the primary button on fine pointers.
- **Black Plate Muted** (black-plate-muted): Secondary text on the Bestiary's rich black: threat labels and definition terms.

### Named Rules
**The Chapter Plate Rule.** Each chapter owns exactly one ink as a full-bleed plate: cyan for the Walkthrough, magenta for Shop Math, rich black for the Bestiary, paper shade for the Appendix. The same ink colors that chapter's thumb tab. A new chapter takes a plate; it does not invent a fifth ink.

**The Overprint Rule.** Where two inks meet they multiply (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`), never stack opaquely: thumb tabs overlap and overprint each other, the magenta season wall overprints the cyan trail, the press-check plates overprint into the title.

**The Reader's Ink Rule.** The highlighter marks only what the reader controls or has entered. A figure the guide derived or estimated never gets it, no matter how important.

**The Press Furniture Rule.** Process Yellow and the extra control-strip patches (the blue, red, green and gray swatches of the colophon color bar) live only in the press-check opening and the color control strips. They are never used as interface color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Anybody (with Archivo, system-ui fallback), weights 800 and 900, `wdth` 50 to 150
**Body Font:** Archivo (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI fallback), weights 400 to 800, `wdth` 86 to 100 in use

**Character:** Anybody is the guide's cover and chapter voice: black, uppercase, and stretched or condensed on its width axis to fill a measure. Archivo is the guide's table and running text: narrower, workmanlike, with tabular lining figures in every number column.

### Named Rules
**The Width Axis Rule.** Display hierarchy is tuned on `wdth`, not just size: titles rest at 125, buttons at 115, tabs at 112, compact mobile tabs at 74, the press-check word at 150. Motion animates `wdth`; it never fakes width with scale.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every number column and every changing figure uses tabular lining figures, so values can roll in place without the column jittering.

## Layout

One strict 12-column grid (`repeat(12, minmax(0, 1fr))`, column gap `gap`) is the only geometry. The title page splits 8/4 (verdict and sentence / stat block); the Walkthrough 3/9 (read-outs / route map); Shop Math 7 + 4 with a one-column gutter (ledger / item tiles); bestiary entries 5/4/3 (name and threat / definition list / "what it means for you"); clear conditions 4 + 7; appendix 7 + 4. Below 1080px every pair stacks to full width, the read-outs become a two-up grid, and bestiary entries become 12 / 7 + 5, then fully stacked under 720px. Each chapter opener sets its dek in columns 1–5 and the "In this chapter" contents in 7–12; under 1080px the dek takes 1–8 and the contents 1–9, and under 720px both run full width.

The container is `.wrap`: full width to 1400px, centered, `gutter` on the left and `gutter + tab-reserve` on the right from 900px up, so content never runs under the thumb tabs. Chapter plates bleed full width outside the wrap; their content sits inside it.

Breakpoints in use: 1080px (grid pairs stack, opener widens), 900px (tabs move from right edge to a sticky top strip, tab reserve drops to 0, colophon stacks; the walkthrough pin also requires min-height 720px), 860px (walkthrough notes stack), 720px (ROI pair, bestiary and opener stack, compare-table tags collapse into a footnote), 620px (method list stacks, masthead trims, running heads show only the recto page, contents rows drop to 0.9375rem), 480px (masthead meta hides).

Vertical rhythm is fluid clamps rather than a step scale: plates pad clamp(14px, 1.8vw, 24px) above the running head and clamp(30px, 3.6vw, 52px) below the opener; the running head's clamp(40px, 7.2vw, 108px) bottom margin holds the chapter title where it always stood. Chapter bodies run clamp(36px, 5vw, 72px) top and clamp(64px, 9vw, 128px) bottom.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Nothing floats and there are no ambient or drop shadows. Depth is conveyed the way print conveys it: by ink layering (multiply overprint), by plate color changing under the reader, by halftone dot ramps that dissolve each plate into the paper below it, by halftone screens that print whatever expires with the season (locked item tiles, the route past the season wall), and by rule weight (1px hairline, 3px rule, 7px heavy rule, 4px double rule under ledger totals).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Ink ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px currentColor`): The ghost button's outline, drawn as an inset ring so it never shifts layout.
- **Hairline ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1.5px var(--ink)`): Cost-per-hour ruler marker labels; 1px variants keyline the paper and black thumb tabs.
- **Field focus** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--ink)`): Focus on appendix tune inputs, replacing the default outline.
- **Misregistration flash** (`text-shadow: -3px -1px 0 rgba(0,166,226,.85), 3px 1px 0 rgba(224,0,122,.8)` to zero over 420ms): Transient only, when a figure changes. Never at rest.

### Named Rules
**The Ink Not Light Rule.** Depth comes from ink layering and rules, never from light. A surface that needs to feel separate gets a plate, a rule, or an overprint, not a shadow.

## Shapes

Square-cut by default: plates, tables, rules and the ledger have no radius. Small radii are functional and graded by role: provenance tags and threat pips at 2px, item tiles, input fields and marker labels at 3px, buttons and checkboxes at 4px, thumb tabs at 7px on their inner edge only (they bleed off the trim). Tier dots, slider thumbs, step buttons and route callout discs are full circles. The scrollbar thumb is an ink slug rounded at 8px (thumb); the ROI strike's ends are eased at .05em (strike). Threat pips are squares rotated 45 degrees into diamonds. The highlighter swipe is the one deliberately irregular shape: uneven radii (.14em .26em .18em .3em, the widest corner recorded as swipe-corner) skewed -7 degrees and rotated -0.7 degrees, like a real marker stroke.

Crop marks are two 1px hairlines pointing out from each trim corner, held off the corner by a 4px gap, clamp(8px, 2vw, 22px) long. Registration targets (crosshair circle with two filled quadrants) serve as the wordmark, the favicon and the press-check corners.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid ink slugs set in display type, quiet and declarative.
- **Shape:** Gently squared (4px radius).
- **Primary:** Rich Black Ink fill, paper text, Anybody 800 uppercase at 0.82rem, `wdth` 115, 0.04em tracking, with an optional leading 1.1em line icon. On the black plate it inverts to paper fill and ink text.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover lifts to Ink Lift only on fine pointers; press scales to 0.97 on `ease-out`; focus is the global 2px ink outline at 3px offset (highlighter on the black plate). Disabled drops to 45% opacity.
- **Ghost:** Transparent with a 2px inset ring in current color; hover fills Paper Tint.

### Provenance Tags
Tiny stamped tickets that say where a number came from.
- **Style:** 0.6875rem Archivo 700 uppercase, 0.06em tracking, 1px border, 2px radius, padding 3px 4px 2px.
- **Variants:** `est.` is a solid ink border; `derived` is a dashed Ink 3 border with Ink 2 text; `yours` is highlighter-filled. Every figure in the stat block, ledger, read-outs, ROI heads and ruler markers carries one.

### Inputs / Fields
The primary action is the sentence itself: "I play [game] [10] hours a week and would actually [buy the premium pass] … [25%]".
- **Select:** Native select with appearance removed, no border, 800 weight, a drawn chevron, auto-sized to its current option's width.

### Motion
Two curves: `ease-out` cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) (GSAP `expo.out`) for nearly everything, and `ease-io` cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1) (`expo.inOut`) for plate travel and trims.
- **Press-check opening (once per session):** crop marks scale in, registration targets spin in, the control strip rises; the yellow, cyan and magenta plates of the title slide 1.3s into register while the word widens to `wdth` 150; black prints, the color plates drop, then the sheet trims upward with a clip-path. A 6s CSS failsafe hides it if script fails; the colophon can replay it.
- **Title entrance:** verdict letters rise from their line masks while misregistered cyan and magenta copies settle and fade; rows of the stat block print left to right; highlighter swipes run 110ms apart.
- **Verdict reprint:** when the verdict changes, the new word reprints with cyan and magenta plates slipping back into register (0.7s).
- **Number roll:** changed figures count to their new value over 0.6s with the misregistration flash.
- **Scroll:** plates wipe in from alternating sides, chapter titles widen 52 to 125 on `wdth`, halftone ramps grow; each opener's dek and contents fade up together (18px, 0.8s `expo.out`, 0.1s stagger) on the plate's trigger; the Walkthrough pins (900px+ wide and 720px+ tall) and scroll plays the season week by week; ledger rows print, tiles flip in, the strike draws, bestiary rules draw and entries re-rank by FLIP. Lenis smooths scrolling.
- **Reduced motion:** no press check, no Lenis, no pins or scroll scenes; highlighters arrive already swiped, halftones are drawn complete, numbers set instantly, and CSS transitions collapse to 0.01ms. The page reads identically, just still.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give each chapter one process ink as a full-bleed plate and the matching thumb tab (cyan walkthrough, magenta shop math, rich black bestiary, paper shade appendix).
- **Do** multiply wherever two inks meet: overlapping tabs, the wall over the trail, plates in register.
- **Do** tag every figure with its provenance: `yours`, `est.`, or `derived`.
- **Do** reserve the highlighter (#e8ff3a) for what the reader controls or has entered.
- **Do** set magenta text in Magenta Text Ink (#c4006b) and white only on the magenta plate.
- **Do** set every number in tabular lining figures and let changed numbers roll and misregister.
- **Do** snap every new block to the 12-column grid inside `.wrap`, keeping the 46px tab reserve from 900px up.
- **Do** separate with rules (1px hairline, 3px, 7px, double) rather than boxes.
- **Do** design the reduced-motion state as a finished still page.

### Don't:
- **Don't** build the dark neon gaming dashboard: no glass cards, no glows, no gradient headings or gradient text.
- **Don't** put a small uppercase eyebrow or kicker above headings; uppercase micro-type is for provenance tags and table column heads only.
- **Don't** print states as pills or badges; print them as sentences in the guide's voice.
- **Don't** use the highlighter on derived or estimated figures, or as a general accent.
- **Don't** use Process Yellow or the control-strip patches outside the press check and color bars.
- **Don't** add drop or ambient shadows; depth is ink, rule and overprint.
- **Don't** introduce a fifth ink or a second display face.
- **Don't** use publisher logos, game art or trade dress.
