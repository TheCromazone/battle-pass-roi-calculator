# Surface: Battle Pass ROI Calculator (index.html)

Scope: the single-page calculator and its behavioral-economics guide. Visitor mode: Experience (the working calculator leads from the first viewport; the page is the artifact).

Audience and job: a player at the start of a season deciding whether to buy the premium pass; a portfolio reviewer judging craft. Action: set game + weekly hours + want-rate, read the verdict, scrub the season. Proof: the user's own numbers, the editable presets (labeled estimates), the 2023 thesis findings with attribution. Constraints: zero-build single HTML file, no publisher marks, neutral (no persuasion), reduced-motion path designed, WCAG AA.

Memorable moment: the press-check opening, where the title prints into register, and the Walkthrough, where dragging the highlighter "you are here" marker through the season lights the tier track and the season-end wall cuts off what you will not reach.

Unresolved: real season lengths and currency refunds per game are user-tunable estimates, not sourced facts.

Competitive alternate (not built): sneaker-box archive wall (holds audience identification through drop culture, loses product clarity).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The season, written up as an official strategy guide for your own playtime: a walkthrough, an item-shop math chapter and a bestiary of the traps. It refuses the category default of a dark neon gaming dashboard with glass cards and gradient headings.

OWN-WORLD: Coated cool-white stock printed in process ink. Cyan, magenta and rich black each own a chapter as full-bleed plates and thumb-index tabs bleeding off the right edge, overprinting by multiply where they meet. Fluorescent highlighter yellow belongs only to the reader's own numbers. Heavy extended display type set on a variable width axis, dense ruled stat tables with tabular figures, crop and registration marks, and halftone dot fields. Every figure carries a provenance tag: yours, est., or derived.
- Raise (sneaker-box wall): one fixed stat-label schema of game, season, price, tiers and hours rules every game entry, like an end label.
- Raise (moon-shadow bazaar): every number carries its origin ticket, so no estimate passes as fact.
- Raise (skate deck wall): one spot ink, the highlighter, marks only what is in the reader's hand.
- Raise (Tropicália sleeve): scale courage. Verdict numerals set enormous against dense small tables.
- Raise (phosphor terminal): states print themselves as sentences in the guide's voice, never pills or badges.
- Raise (ANSI BBS): one strict column grid is the only geometry. Tabs, tables and plates all snap to it.

STORY: The visitor names their game and hours on the title page, gets the verdict as one printed sentence, scrubs the season walkthrough to see where the wall falls, sees sticker value collapse to real value in the shop math, meets the traps that apply to them in the bestiary, and can tune every assumption in the appendix.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The title page. A giant two-line verdict word (WORTH IT / CLOSE CALL / PROBABLY NOT) in extended black display type takes the left two-thirds, with a sentence-form input set in large type beneath it ("I play [game] [10] hours a week and would actually use [25%] of the cosmetics", inputs highlighted). The right third holds the season stat block (tier reached, finish week, cost per hour, real ROI) as a ruled table. Crop marks frame the trim and thumb tabs sit at the right edge. The primary action is the sentence inputs themselves.

FORM: The official strategy guide (BradyGames/Prima publication tradition), position 7 of 7 on my ordered grounded list; seed key 2c9a88ef.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION + MOTION GRAMMAR: The press-check opening: CMYK plates slide into register, crop marks draw, then the sheet trims. In the Walkthrough, a draggable highlighter marker scrubs weeks across a route map of the tier track, with tiers lighting and the season-end wall falling. Changed figures jump out of register and snap back. Chapter titles expand along the width axis as you scroll in. Exponential ease-out throughout, GSAP timelines plus ScrollTrigger, and a designed reduced-motion path.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
