    From the thesis

    • 200–300%How much players typically overvalue a cosmetic once they've unlocked it (endowment effect).
    • 40–60%The extra players pay for time-gated items over permanently available ones that look the same.
    • 26%Higher satisfaction among players who complete 70% or more of a pass, whatever their ROI.
    • ~35%Drop in impulsive loot box spending after battle passes arrived, with steadier seasonal spending in its place.

    Method

    Hours per tier
    Hours to finish ÷ tiers
    Tier at season end
    Lesser of all tiers and ⌊(hours a week × season weeks) ÷ hours per tier⌋
    Sticker value
    Cosmetics × average item price
    Real value
    Cosmetics unlocked by season end × average item price × share you'd use
    Your ROI
    (Real value − pass price) ÷ pass price
    ROI on the box
    (Sticker value − pass price) ÷ pass price
    Cost per hour
    Pass price ÷ hours played while the pass still pays out
    Verdict
    Worth it when you finish and the real value covers the price. Probably not when neither holds. Close call otherwise.

    Progress is modeled as steady per hour of play. XP events and weekly challenges usually speed it up, so read the forecast as a floor.
